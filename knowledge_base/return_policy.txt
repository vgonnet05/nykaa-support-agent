Nykaa Return Policy — Project Assumptions

For this project, eligible Apparel, Footwear, and Home products may be returned within 7 days of delivery, while eligible Electronics products may be returned within 7 days subject to product-specific conditions. Beauty products are not eligible for change-of-mind returns, but damaged or defective items may qualify for a claim. Eligible returns qualify for reverse pickup only when the delivery address is serviceable and pickup is available. These timelines and conditions are fictional project assumptions for testing and are not verified official Nykaa policies.
